== Description ==

**Customer Engagement System (CES) by Supsystic** turns visitors into contacts and contacts into customers. A popup builder, a form builder, a CRM and email marketing automation share one contact base inside your WordPress, so an exit-intent popup can capture an email, the form tags the contact, and the right follow-up email goes out on its own. No Zapier, no syncing between services, no per-contact fees.

Popups, forms and contacts are unlimited, and your contacts stay in your own WordPress database.

**Try it in your browser:** [open a live demo in WordPress Playground](https://playground.wordpress.net/?plugin=popup-by-supsystic) – no install, no account.

= From visitor to customer in five steps =

1. **Capture** – a popup with an offer appears at the right moment: on exit intent, after a scroll or delay, or on a click.
2. **Collect** – the form inside the popup saves the visitor as a contact in your CRM.
3. **Organize** – tags, custom fields and an engagement score show who is ready to buy.
4. **Follow up** – a tag starts an email automation: a welcome email, a reminder, an offer.
5. **Sell** – every order is credited to the email, popup or form that brought it in.

Each step writes to the same contact, so your sales funnel builds itself and you can see where every customer came from.

= Four modules, one contact base =

* **Popup** – modals, slide-ins, bars and full-screen welcome mats with 10+ triggers and 10+ targeting rules.
* **Forms** – contact, lead, newsletter, quote, login and signup forms with conditional logic and spam protection.
* **CRM** – contacts, tags, custom fields and a timeline of everything each contact did on your site.
* **Email Campaigns & Automations** – newsletters, welcome series and follow-ups started by tags, with open, click and revenue tracking.

Every module writes to the same contact. A popup view, a form submit, an email click and a WooCommerce order all end up on one record.

== Installation ==

1. In your dashboard go to Plugins → Add New Plugin and search for "Customer Engagement System".
2. Click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Open **Customer Engagement** in the admin menu.
4. Pick a play on the Plays screen, or create a popup or form from a template.
5. Under Settings › Email sending, choose PHP mail or your SMTP server before you switch on email automations.

For scheduled emails on a live site, a real server cron that calls `wp-cron.php` every few minutes gives the most reliable timing.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What is the Customer Engagement System? =

It is a WordPress plugin with four connected modules – Popup, Forms, CRM and Email Campaigns & Automations – that share one contact base. It grew out of Popup by Supsystic.

= Do I have to learn all four modules before I start? =

No. Open Plays, choose the outcome you want and answer a few questions. CES builds the popup, form, contact tags and emails together. Every piece opens in its full editor when you want more control.

= Where is my contact data stored? =

In your own WordPress database. Nothing is sent to Supsystic. You can export every contact to CSV at any time.

= Is there a limit on contacts or popups? =

No. Popups, forms and contacts are unlimited, and contacts are never counted or charged.

= Do I still need Mailchimp, Brevo or a similar service? =

Not for lists, newsletters or automations – CES handles those. You only need something that delivers the mail: PHP mail, your own SMTP server, or a direct connection to a provider such as Brevo, SendGrid, Mailgun or Amazon SES.

= Does it work with WooCommerce? =

Yes. Customers and orders, including guest orders, are imported into the CRM with total spent and average order value, popups can appear after checkout, and automations can follow up on real orders.

= Does it have exit-intent popups and spin-to-win wheels? =

Yes. Exit intent is one of the built-in triggers, and the template library includes spin-to-win wheels, scratch cards, mystery boxes and other gamified popups.

= Can I use the forms without a popup? =

Yes. Every form can be embedded on any page or post with a block or shortcode, or placed inside a popup.

= Will it slow down my site? =

Popups are rendered only on the pages where their targeting rules match.

= Does it work with my theme and page builder? =

Yes. Popups and forms work with modern WordPress themes, and the shortcodes work in any page builder.

= How can I report a security issue? =

Please report security issues through the [Patchstack Vulnerability Disclosure Program](https://patchstack.com/database/vdp/9e5fb5a0-2ab7-489d-9ef9-d0240885254b). The Patchstack team validates and triages each report and notifies us.

== External services ==

Customer Engagement System connects to the services below only for the features that use them.

* **Google Fonts** – only when a popup or form uses a Google font. The visitor's browser loads the font from `fonts.googleapis.com`. [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

* Forms with **Google reCAPTCHA** switched on send the visitor's CAPTCHA response to Google to verify the submission ([privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [terms](https://policies.google.com/terms)).
